# Problem Statement

## 1. Background & Context
Consumer spending across major lifestyle milestones—such as moving into a new home, hosting an event (birthday, wedding, anniversary), or purchasing occasion-specific jewelry—is characterized by high financial variance, cognitive overload, and fragmented purchasing workflows. In India, consumer spending in these categories represents substantial portions of disposable household income, yet the tools available to assist consumers remain rudimentary.

## 2. Core Problem Dimensions

### 2.1 The Budget Allocation Dilemma
- Consumers usually know their **top-line budget** (e.g., ₹50,000 for a living room upgrade, ₹1,00,000 for a wedding reception party, or ₹25,000 for festival jewelry), but have difficulty calculating how much to allocate across subcategories (e.g., lighting vs. furniture vs. appliances; catering vs. venue vs. entertainment).
- Without proportional guidance, consumers frequently overspend on initial items (e.g., an expensive sofa or premium venue) and are left with insufficient funds for critical necessities (e.g., ceiling fans, lighting, or catering).

### 2.2 Sourcing & Platform Fragmentation
- Unlike unified commerce platforms, finding the right products within budget requires searching across 5 to 10 separate apps:
  - **Home Goods**: Amazon India, Flipkart, IKEA India, Myntra, Ajio.
  - **Party & Catering**: Swiggy, Zomato, BigBasket, BookMyShow, OYO, Booking.com, MakeMyTrip.
  - **Jewelry**: Tanishq, CaratLane, BlueStone, Melorra, Meesho.
- Manually formulating search queries, comparing prices, and verifying item-by-item budget fits is exhausting and time-consuming.

### 2.3 Style & Occasion Disconnect in Jewelry
- Buying jewelry without visual styling context often leads to poor outfit-to-ornament pairing (e.g., conflicting metals, clashing color palettes, or mismatched formality levels).
- Standard e-commerce filters cannot evaluate an uploaded outfit photograph to deduce color synergy, design aesthetics, or appropriate jewelry types.

### 2.4 Lack of Offline Reliability in Modern GenAI Tools
- Pure LLM-dependent tools frequently suffer from API rate limiting, cold-start latency, token cost surges, or sudden network disconnects. A budgeting application must remain reliably responsive even without active third-party cloud API keys.

## 3. Summary of Target Pain Points
1. **Unstructured Budgeting**: Inability to mathematically divide budgets by sensible lifestyle ratios.
2. **Time Wastage**: Hours lost manually copy-pasting search queries across diverse Indian retail apps.
3. **Mismatched Aesthetic Choices**: Difficulty picking jewelry that harmonizes with a specific dress or occasion.
4. **Fragile Cloud Dependency**: Failure of apps when AI provider APIs experience downtime or lack credentials.
